Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to 1on1chess.com ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our tabletop chess application.

2. Information We Collect

Our application is designed to minimize data collection. We collect the following information:

  • Game State Data: Your chess game state (board position, timer values, move history) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This may include information like your device type, browser type, and general usage patterns.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may collect cookies and other data for ad personalization and measurement.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Game Functionality: localStorage data is used solely to save your game progress and preferences on your device.
  • Analytics: We use analytics to improve our service and understand user behavior.
  • Advertising: We display ads to support the free operation of this website.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements. Google Ads Policy
  • Firebase: For hosting our website.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: localStorage is used to save your game state.
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics uses cookies to track website usage.
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense uses cookies for ad personalization and measurement.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling localStorage will prevent the game from saving your progress.

6. Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not store any game data on our servers. If you clear your browser data or localStorage, your game progress will be lost.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game data stored in localStorage does not contain any personally identifiable information.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your game data at any time using your browser's localStorage clearing function
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect when changes were made. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository: github.com/moshebeeri/1on1chess
