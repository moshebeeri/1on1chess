Free Tabletop Chess Game for Tablets and Mobile Phones

The Best Chess Solution for Travelers - Works Offline!

1on1chess.com is the perfect digital chess board replacement for your iPad, tablet, or mobile phone. Designed specifically for tabletop play, this free chess application is ideal for travelers and anyone who wants to play chess without carrying a physical board.

Key Features:

  • Works completely offline - no internet connection required
  • Built-in chess clock with multiple time controls (Bullet, Blitz, Rapid, Classical)
  • Automatic game state saving - never lose your game
  • Perfect for tablets and mobile devices (iPad, iPhone, Android)
  • Designed for tabletop play - both players can see the board
  • Move history and PGN notation tracking
  • No installation required - works in your browser
  • Free and open source

Replace your chess board with your tablet. Perfect for travel, coffee shops, parks, or anywhere you want to play chess. The best offline chess clock and board combination for mobile devices.
